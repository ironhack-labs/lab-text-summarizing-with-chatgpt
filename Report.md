# My Exercise Report – Text Summarizing with ChatGPT

## Part 1: What I Tried

I took a CDJ-3000 review and asked GPT to make three different kinds of summaries.

### 1. Bullet List Version
Good stuff:
- Bigger screen
- Better jog wheel
- Extra loop and beat jump buttons
- EQ waveform

Bad stuff:
- Hot cues are in a bad spot
- Pitch fader is hard to set right
- Screen freezes sometimes (no multitouch)
- Some buttons feel old and clunky
- Player froze twice

---

### 2. Professional Paragraph
The CDJ-3000 adds a bigger screen, nicer jog wheel, and better looping features. But there are problems like bad hot cue placement, shaky pitch fader, no multitouch, and even freezing during use. Some buttons also feel old and not needed.

---

### 3. Casual Style
The CDJ-3000 is cool with the bigger screen and smoother jog. Beat jump and EQ display are nice too. But the hot cues are annoying, pitch fader is fiddly, and the screen freezing sucks. Still better than before, just not perfect.

---

## Part 2: What I Saw

- Bullets are the clearest and easiest.  
- The professional one feels more “official.”  
- The casual one is fun but leaves stuff out sometimes.  

---

## Part 3: Did GPT Mess Up?

- It didn’t make up fake details (no hallucinations this time).  
- Word count rules weren’t always exact. For example, “under 120 words” was close, but not perfect.  

---

## Part 4: What I Learned

- How you write the prompt changes the style a lot.  
- Bullet points are quick and simple.  
- Paragraphs are good if you need to sound serious.  
- Casual tone works for fun chats, but not for reports.  
- GPT tries to follow word rules, but it’s not always exact.  

---

✅ In short: different prompts = different vibes. That’s the main lesson I got.
